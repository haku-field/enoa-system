# Observable Manifestation

**Version:** 0.1-alpha  
**Status:** Alpha Domain Specification

## 1. Scope

This specification describes how an existence, its state, or its contour may become observable under particular observational conditions.

The alpha focuses on the general distinction between an existence and its manifestation.

## 2. Manifestation

A **Manifestation** is an observable appearance or expression through which some state, contour, or effect of an existence becomes available under particular observational conditions.

A manifestation is not the existence itself and does not necessarily reveal the complete contour or internal state of that existence.

```text
Existence ≠ Contour ≠ Manifestation
```

## 3. Observation conditions

Manifestation may vary with viewpoint, distance, scale, medium, environment, time, surrounding relations, available sensors, or representation method.

The same existence may therefore produce different observable manifestations under different conditions.

## 4. Manifestation and identification

A manifestation may provide differences that function as cues for a Focal Point or contribute to reconstruction of a Contour.

```text
Manifestation ≠ Focal Point
Manifestation ≠ complete Contour
```

Multiple manifestations may contribute to attribution toward the same target where sufficient reconnective conditions are present.

## 5. Domain extensions

Visual manifestation may be described through specialized tools such as Image Design Language / Shouei.

Other media may require their own domain-specific extensions.

The alpha does not assume that one manifestation language is sufficient for all observational domains.
