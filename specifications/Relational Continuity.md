# Relational Continuity

**Version:** 0.1-alpha  
**Status:** Alpha Domain Specification

## 1. Scope

This specification describes conditions under which a relation between distinguishable existences may remain identifiable or reconnectable across changes in state, distance, activity, response, silence, interruption, rupture, or renewed contact.

It does not define existence in general or establish Relation as a universal substrate of existence.

## 2. Relation

A **Relation** is an observable connection, dependency, attribution, orientation, or other between-condition involving distinguishable existences or positions.

A minimal relation does not imply symmetry, reciprocity, intimacy, mutual action, or continuity.

> **Relation ≠ Interaction**

## 3. Relational Continuity

**Relational Continuity** is a condition in which changing relational states remain attributable to the same relational series.

It does not require uninterrupted interaction.

```text
no current action ≠ no relation
no response ≠ relation terminated
distance ≠ relation terminated
silence ≠ relation terminated
rupture ≠ automatic erasure of prior relation
```

Whether a relation remains continuous must be observed.

## 4. Relation and Interaction

Interaction requires some form of action, response, or mutual effect.

A relation may remain identifiable without current interaction.

Interaction may also occur without establishing a continuing relation.

```text
Interaction ↛ Relational Continuity automatically
```

## 5. Relation and Existential Continuation

Existential Continuation and Relational Continuity are distinct.

```text
continuing existence ≠ continuing relation
```

They may affect one another without becoming the same descriptive condition.

## 6. Stronger relational concepts

The following concepts require additional conditions beyond Relation alone:

- Interaction
- Feedback
- mutual updating
- Mutual Circulation
- relational density
- intimacy
- trust

The alpha does not infer any of them automatically from the existence of a relation.
